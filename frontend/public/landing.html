EST. FEBRUARY 2014

One simple act.
A lifetime of
bear memories.

On February 22, 2014, Rob Tippenhauer helped his karate instructor at a small sushi restaurant in Kentucky. As a thank-you, that instructor introduced Rob to Chuy's. The very next Tuesday Rob was back — hooked on the free queso bar. His husband Terry Wachtman walked in at 7:00 PM after Tai Chi class, and neither of them left.

Within a few weeks, two other Bears — TJVBear and OhioBear — started joining regularly. Before long, Terry was spending his afternoons reaching out to everyone for a headcount, so they created a Facebook event to track attendance. By fall 2015, the group had grown so much they needed real events to keep track of everyone. On February 21, 2016 — almost two years to the day from that first Kentucky dinner — the DinnerBears community was officially born. After months of Dayton friends making the drive south for Tuesday nights, they earned their own night: the first monthly Dayton dinner launched August 3, 2016, and has run every month since. What started as Rob at the queso bar has grown into two cities, two nights, and a lifetime of friendships.

"It's amazing what one simple act of helping someone in a time of need has led to in a few short years."
