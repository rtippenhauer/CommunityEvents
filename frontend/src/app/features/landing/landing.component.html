Your community, your site

Every community runs on its own address with its own name, colours, wording and artwork. Its members, events and history belong to it and are visible to nobody else. A community can bring its own domain, its own sign-in and its own email account, or use the ones this deployment provides.

Have a look around

The demo is a working community with events, members and a leaderboard already in it. Sign up there and you land as an admin, so you see the organiser's side rather than just the visitor's.
